I got into cybersecurity after a friend's WordPress site got compromised and I spent a weekend figuring out how the attacker got in. That rabbit hole turned into a habit — I now spend most evenings in TryHackMe rooms, reading writeups, or picking apart CTF challenges.

I'm currently focused on web application security and the fundamentals of networking and Active Directory, with Python as my go-to for writing quick tooling. Longer term I'm aiming for a role on a blue team, but I like understanding the offensive side well enough to know what I'm defending against.

This site is where I log what I'm learning, show the tools I've built, and keep track of the skills I'm building room by room.
