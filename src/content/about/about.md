It started with a pile of broken toys.

As a kid, my bedroom floor was covered in screws, springs, and tiny circuit boards. I had to know what was inside everything, even the TV remote. I wasn't just taking things apart. I was hoping to build something new from the pieces.

That curiosity never left. It grew with shows like "How It's Made" and "How the Universe Works", and then the internet became the biggest "how does this work?" of all. One YouTube video at a time, I uncovered networks, packets, and the hidden journey of data.

Then I found the darker side: hacking, the dark web, and the field built to defend against it, cybersecurity.

I chose the blue team. Today, I'm solving TryHackMe labs and gaining hands-on SOC experience, learning to detect, investigate, and stop threats.

This site is where I log what I'm learning, show the tools I've built, and keep track of the skills I'm building room by room.
